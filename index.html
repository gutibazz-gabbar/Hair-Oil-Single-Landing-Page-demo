Origin & sourcing

Where every drop comes from

Each ingredient has a traditional place in Ayurvedic and South Asian hair care. We buy directly from named farming communities, so we can trace it, test it and pay growers fairly.

🥥

Virgin coconut oil

📍 Barishal & Patuakhali coast

The base oil. Rich in lauric acid, which penetrates the hair shaft and reduces protein loss. Cold-pressed from sun-dried copra.

🫒

Amla (Indian gooseberry)

📍 Rajshahi orchards

A traditional source of vitamin C and antioxidants, used for centuries to support scalp health and natural colour.

🌼

Bhringraj

📍 Sylhet & Moulvibazar hills

Called the “king of herbs” for hair in Ayurveda. Used to support growth and reduce thinning.

🍃

Neem leaf

📍 Chapainawabganj

Naturally clarifying. Helps keep the scalp free of dandruff and itch.

⚫

Black seed (Kalonji)

📍 Faridpur & Jashore farms

Cold-pressed seed oil rich in thymoquinone, valued for strengthening follicles.

🌺

Hibiscus & fenugreek

📍 Gazipur & Bogura

Add slip, shine and softness, and support thicker-looking strands.

How we made it

Slow-made in small batches

No shortcuts, no heat shocks, no fillers. From farm to bottle takes about 21 days.

Harvest & inspect

Herbs are hand-picked at peak potency, then checked for pesticide residue before they enter our unit.

Sun-dry & clean

Leaves and flowers are dried in shade and sorted by hand. Nothing is bleached or sprayed.

Cold-press

Coconut and black seed are pressed below 40°C so heat-sensitive nutrients survive.

Slow infusion

Herbs steep in the oil for 14 days under gentle warmth, drawing out their active compounds.

Filter & test

Triple-filtered, then tested in a lab for purity and microbes. Each batch gets a number.

Bottle

Packed in UV-protective glass so the oil stays fresh without synthetic preservatives.

Our farming story

It started with a grandmother’s oiling ritual.

Our founder grew up watching weekend hair-oiling with oil pressed at home. Years later, the shop-bought bottles smelled of perfume and left hair heavier, not healthier. So we went back to the source: the coconut groves of the southern coast, the hill herb gardens of Sylhet and the orchards of the north.

We work with a small group of partner growers who farm without synthetic pesticides. We buy at the farm gate, pay on time and visit often, and we only buy what is in season, so every batch has a farm behind it.

  • Direct farm-gate buying, with no middlemen
  • Fair, on-time payment to growers
  • Seasonal harvests and sun-drying only
Our factory setup

A small, clean unit built for small batches.

Each stage is kept separate and checked. Staff wear gloves, hair nets and aprons, and every surface the oil touches is stainless steel or food-grade. Each batch gets its own number and record sheet.

  1. Cold press: coconut and black seed pressed below 40°C
  2. Infusion tanks: herbs steep in the oil for 14 days
  3. Filtration: triple-filtered for clarity
  4. Lab corner: samples tested before release
  5. Bottling: UV-protective glass, labelled with MFG and EXP dates

Why this is organic

Organic is a standard, not a label

We hold ourselves to the principles behind recognised organic certification.

  • Grown without synthetic pesticides, herbicides or chemical fertiliser
  • No GMO seeds, and no irradiation of raw material
  • Extracted mechanically: no hexane or chemical solvents
  • Free from mineral oil, parabens, sulphates, silicones and artificial fragrance
  • Farm-to-bottle batch traceability

The Bonoja ritual

Five minutes. Every other night.

Oiling should feel like a pause, not a chore. Warm a little oil, comb it through, and let it work while you rest.

1

Warm a few drops

Rub a teaspoon of oil between your palms, or fill your wooden comb.

2

Comb from roots to tips

Work in sections with slow strokes. The comb spreads oil evenly and detangles gently.

3

Massage your scalp

Use your fingertips in small circles for 3–5 minutes.

4

Rest, then rinse

Leave for an hour or overnight, then wash with a mild shampoo.

FAQ

Questions, answered


How often should I use it?

Massage into the scalp 2–3 times a week. Leave on for at least 1 hour or overnight, then wash with a mild shampoo.

How long until I see results?

Most people notice less breakage and better shine in 4–6 weeks, and less hair fall in 8–12 weeks. Results vary.

Is it safe for coloured or chemically treated hair?

Yes. It contains no harsh chemicals. Do a patch test first.

Why does the oil thicken or change colour?

Coconut oil solidifies below about 24°C. Warm the bottle in lukewarm water. Natural herb batches may vary slightly in shade.

How does the refillable wooden comb work?

Unscrew the cap, pour in about 10 ml of Bonoja oil, and close it. As you comb, the oil seeps through fine channels in the teeth. Wash it with a damp cloth. Do not soak it.

How should I store it?

Keep away from direct sunlight, tightly closed. Best used within 12 months of the batch date.
